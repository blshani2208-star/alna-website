Your organisation is the starting point

ALNA connects documents, operational data and staff perspectives to understand where a process becomes difficult, what might explain it and which courses of action fit your organisation.

1 · Understand the context

Map your stages, roles, terminology, resources and constraints.

2 · Connect the evidence

Compare documents and operational data with staff perspectives from questionnaires and focused clarification.

3 · Examine explanations

Separate observed findings from possible explanations and missing information.

4 · Develop options

Present possible actions, their basis and limitations to support your decision.

How are findings examined?

Options are examined against the available evidence, fit with the population and process, resources, staff workload, risks and the ability to measure change. Competing explanations and missing information are made explicit. Professional knowledge and relevant sources inform the review; this does not assume that every recommendation is automatically generated from a research database.

Start with one challenge

An introductory conversation explores what concerns you, what you have already tried and what you need to understand before deciding on a next step. Scope and timing are agreed around the process, available materials and readiness to begin.
