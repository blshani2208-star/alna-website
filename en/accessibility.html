Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 2026

At ALNA, we place great importance on providing equal and accessible service to all visitors to this site, including people with disabilities. This site was built with a commitment to accessibility in line with the WCAG 2.0 Level AA guidelines, and, for our Hebrew-language site, the Israeli Standard IS 5568 for internet content accessibility and Israel's Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013.

Accessibility measures implemented on this site

  • Correct language and reading-direction markup (English, left-to-right) on every page of this section.
  • Semantic structure of headings, navigation regions, main content, and page footer.
  • A "Skip to main content" link at the start of every page, intended for keyboard users and screen-reader users.
  • Full keyboard navigation and operability for all interactive components, including the FAQ menu.
  • Descriptive alternative text (alt) for meaningful images, with decorative elements marked so they do not interfere with screen readers.
  • Color contrast chosen with the intent of meeting minimum contrast requirements, and content that can be enlarged in the browser without loss of content or functionality.
  • Decorative animations are limited and are not essential to understanding the content; users with a system "reduce motion" preference (prefers-reduced-motion) will see the site with minimal animation.

Conformance level

This site was rebuilt with the aim of conforming to WCAG 2.0 Level AA. Some parts may not yet be fully accessible, and we continue to work on improving accessibility on an ongoing basis.

Accessibility inquiries

If you encounter an accessibility problem or difficulty on this site, we would welcome you contacting us, and we will work to address it as soon as possible.
