Is there a service delivery challenge worth examining more closely?

No long form and no commitment. These questions can help you decide whether a focused diagnostic conversation would be useful.

Do people wait, disengage, or drop out at a particular stage?

Is there a gap between the documented process and day-to-day practice?

Do leaders and frontline teams describe the challenge differently?

Do you have data, but struggle to turn it into a clear decision?

Have changes been introduced without a clear way to learn what worked?

Is it unclear whether workload, ownership, resources, skills, or tools are shaping the problem?

Is it difficult to connect organizational performance with outcomes for people using the service?

If more than one question resonates, there may be value in looking closer. You do not need to know the cause in advance. The diagnostic process is designed to examine plausible explanations with you.
